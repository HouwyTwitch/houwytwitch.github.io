CS.Money
Auto Trade

Replicates the logic of the official CS.Money Chrome extension in pure Python. Automatically processes sale offers, creates Steam trade offers, and confirms them via Steam Guard.

PythonRSA-6144 CS.Money APIasyncio Steam Trade API
View on GitHub ↗
Overview

What it does

When a buyer purchases your CS.Money listing, this tool handles everything automatically. It reverse-engineers the CS.Money Chrome extension flow - including the RSA-6144 session encryption.

Sessions and cookies are persisted to disk so restarts don't require a new login. Rate-limit responses are handled gracefully.

How it works

Automated offer flow

01
Notification polling
Polls every poll_interval seconds. On OFFER_BOUGHT, immediately checks trades.
02
Fetch active offers
Polls active-offers every 30s as a safety net.
03
Notify & send trade
For each CREATING-state offer: notifies CS.Money, then sends the Steam trade offer.
04
Encrypt & report
Fetches RSA-6144 public key, encrypts session, reports trade ID back to CS.Money.
05
Steam Guard confirm
Confirms the trade offer via mobile authenticator using identity_secret.
